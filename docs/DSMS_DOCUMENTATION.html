Part 1: Admin Operational Manual & Module Specifications

1. Getting Started & Authentication

The Driving School Management System (DSMS) provides administrators with a centralized control center to manage student accounts, instructor assignments, driving session scheduling, package offerings, and payment processing.

  • App Access: Open the application to reach the Sign In screen.
  • Authentication: Sign in with your registered administrator email address and password. Enable Remember Me to maintain an active session across launches.
  • Password Reset: In case of forgotten credentials, tap Forgot Password, enter your registered email, verify the OTP code, and set a new secure password.
